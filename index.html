Research

My work is on graph mining and data management: finding cohesive subgraphs, answering community search queries, and measuring how stable a network stays when its edges fail.

Recent projects extend these questions past the simple graph — into bipartite networks and hypergraphs, where the classic definitions of density stop being the right ones, and into billion-scale settings where an exact answer costs more than it is worth.
